Privacy policy

How Azeli Technologies and Azeli PMS collect, use and protect personal data. Last updated 27 November 2026.

1. Who we are

Azeli Technologies ("Azeli", "we") provides hotel marketing services and Azeli PMS, a cloud hotel management system used by hotels such as Amaya Hotel & Luxury Suites. You can reach us at support@azelitech.com or +27 78 709 4814.

This policy covers two situations:

  • Our own website and sales (azelitech.com): here Azeli decides how your data is used.
  • Hotels using Azeli PMS, including their WhatsApp, booking engine and payments: here the hotel is responsible for its guests' data and Azeli processes it on the hotel's behalf and on its instructions. Questions about a stay should go to the hotel first; we will help them answer.

2. What we collect

  • Website enquiries: your name, email, phone, business name and message when you use our contact form, email or WhatsApp us.
  • Hotel guests (through Azeli PMS): name, contact details, nationality and ID details the hotel must record, stay dates, room, requests, bills and payments.
  • WhatsApp: when you message a hotel that uses Azeli PMS on WhatsApp, we receive your WhatsApp phone number, profile name, the messages and files you send, and delivery and read status. The hotel's replies are stored with them.
  • Payments: online payments are handled by Pesapal. Azeli PMS never stores full card numbers or security codes; for cards passed on by booking sites it keeps only the last four digits, card type, holder name and the secure card store's reference.
  • Hotel staff: name, username, role, sign-in times and an activity log, so the hotel can see who did what.

3. How we use it

  • To answer enquiries and manage bookings, stays, bills and payments.
  • To send messages you asked for or agreed to: booking confirmations, pre-arrival information and thank-you messages. Marketing messages on WhatsApp are only sent to guests who agreed to them.
  • To keep the system secure, prevent fraud and meet tax and legal record-keeping duties.
  • To help hotel staff with an AI assistant that summarises information already in the system. It reads; it does not make decisions about you.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use guests' data for our own advertising.

4. WhatsApp messages

Hotels connect to WhatsApp through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. Messages pass through Meta under WhatsApp's Business Policy and are stored in the hotel's Azeli PMS so staff can reply. Reply STOP at any time and the hotel's automatic messages to you stop; reply START to receive them again.

5. Who we share it with

Only with the services needed to run the system, each bound to protect the data:

  • Render (hosting and database, EU region), Vercel and Cloudflare (website and domain)
  • Meta / WhatsApp (messaging), Zoho and Brevo (email)
  • Pesapal (payments) and Channex (connection to Booking.com, Expedia, Agoda and similar sites)
  • Anthropic (the AI assistant used by hotel staff); data sent to it is not used to train its models
  • Authorities, when the law requires it

Some of these services are outside Uganda. We use them only where the data stays protected.

6. How long we keep it

Website enquiries: up to 2 years after our last contact. Guest and financial records: as long as the hotel needs them and tax law requires (usually up to 7 years). WhatsApp conversations: as long as the hotel keeps the guest's record. Encrypted backups are kept for a limited period and then deleted.

7. How we protect it

Encrypted connections (HTTPS), individual staff accounts with role-based access, two-step sign-in for managers, an audit log of changes, and encrypted nightly backups. Hotels can also limit staff sign-in to the hotel's own Wi-Fi.

8. Your rights and how to delete your data

Under Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 and similar laws, you can ask to see the data held about you, correct it, object to marketing, or have it deleted.

To ask for your data to be deleted (including your WhatsApp messages):

  1. Email support@azelitech.com with the subject "Data deletion request", or send "DELETE MY DATA" on WhatsApp to the hotel's number.
  2. Tell us your name, the phone number or email you used, and the hotel concerned.
  3. We confirm your identity, delete or anonymise your data with the hotel within 30 days, and tell you when it is done. Records the law requires us to keep (such as tax invoices) are kept only for that purpose.

9. Changes and contact

We will update this page if our practices change and show the new date at the top. For any privacy question or complaint, contact support@azelitech.com. You may also complain to Uganda's Personal Data Protection Office.

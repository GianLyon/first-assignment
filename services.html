01

OTA Listing & Optimisation

Online travel agencies (OTAs) like Booking.com, Expedia, Airbnb and TripAdvisor are often where guests discover you first. We set up and fine-tune your listings so you appear higher in search results, stand out from nearby competitors and turn more views into bookings.

Why it matters

OTA search rankings reward listings that are complete, accurate and active. Small things like missing amenities, weak photos, slow review replies or prices that don't match across sites quietly push you down the list. We fix them, then keep monitoring so you stay near the top.

What's included

  • Full listing audit and content rewrite
  • Photo selection and ordering for each platform
  • Room types, amenities, facilities and policies completed
  • Rate, availability and price-parity checks
  • Promotions, mobile rates and loyalty programmes (e.g. Booking.com Genius)
  • Review responses and reputation monitoring
  • Monthly ranking, views and conversion report

02

Channel Management

A channel manager connects your property management system (PMS) to every OTA and your own website, so rates and availability update everywhere at once. We choose the right one for you, set it up and run it day to day.

Why it matters

Without one, you're updating each OTA extranet by hand, which leads to overbookings, wrong prices and lost revenue. Properly configured, a booking on one channel instantly closes that room everywhere else, and you can change prices across every site in seconds.

What's included

  • Channel manager recommendation and set-up
  • Connection to your PMS and booking engine
  • Room and rate mapping across every OTA
  • Rate plans, minimum stays and restrictions
  • Rate-parity monitoring
  • Channel mix and commission (cost-of-sale) reviews
  • Adding new channels as you grow

SEO & GEO

SEO (search engine optimisation) helps you rank on Google when people search for places to stay or eat. GEO (generative engine optimisation) helps AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity recommend you when guests ask them for suggestions.

Why it matters

Organic search brings in bookings without paying per click or per booking. And as more travellers plan trips by asking AI, being the property those tools recommend is becoming as important as ranking on Google.

What's included

  • Technical SEO audit and fixes

  • Google Business Profile and Google Maps optimisation
  • Consistent details across local listings and directories
  • Content that answers real guest questions
  • GEO: structured data and AI-ready information
  • Review strategy and monthly ranking reports

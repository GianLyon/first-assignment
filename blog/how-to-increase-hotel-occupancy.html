How to increase occupancy for your hotel

Azeli Technologies · 20 November 2026 · 9 min read

Occupancy is the share of your available rooms that are sold. It sounds simple, but raising it without cutting your price to the bone takes work on several fronts at once. Here are twelve steps we use with hotels, in the order we usually tackle them.

Start with the numbers

1. Know your occupancy by day of the week and by month

An average of 55% hides a lot. Many city hotels are full Monday to Thursday and empty at weekends; many lodges are the opposite. Look at the last twelve months split by weekday and month. The gaps tell you which guests to go after.

2. Know where your bookings come from

Split bookings by channel: walk-in, phone, WhatsApp, website, each OTA, companies, travel agents. If one OTA brings more than half of your rooms, you are exposed to its rules and its commission. Azeli PMS shows this on the dashboard and warns you when one channel gets too large.

Be found

3. Fix your OTA listings first

For most hotels in East Africa, OTAs are where guests first see you. A complete listing with good photos, accurate room names, every amenity ticked and a clear cancellation policy ranks higher and converts better. We cover this in detail in OTA management made easy.

4. Own your Google presence

Claim and complete your Google Business Profile: photos, opening hours, phone, WhatsApp, website and booking link. Answer every review, good or bad. Guests increasingly ask AI assistants for recommendations, and those assistants draw on the same public information.

5. Make your website book rooms

A website without a booking button sends guests back to the OTA. Add a booking engine connected to live availability, show your best rate, and make it work on a phone with a slow connection.

Turn lookers into bookers

6. Answer WhatsApp in minutes, not hours

In Uganda and much of Africa, WhatsApp is where enquiries arrive. Speed wins. Put WhatsApp on a shared inbox rather than one phone, so whoever is on shift can answer. Azeli PMS also reads each conversation for buying signals, such as dates, number of guests, a question about paying a deposit, and marks it as explorer, interested, buyer or ready to book, so the hottest leads are answered first.

7. Make the next step obvious

Every reply should end with a clear next step: a price for their dates, a link to book, or a hold on a room until a set time. "Let us know" loses bookings.

Price for demand

8. Use more than one rate

One price all year leaves money on the table when you are busy and rooms empty when you are not. Set seasonal prices, a slightly cheaper non-refundable rate, a minimum stay over peak dates, and a long-stay rate. Change prices a few weeks ahead, not on the day.

Fill the quiet days

9. Go after corporate and NGO business

Organisations need rooms on weekdays, often for many nights, and they rebook. Agree a company rate, give them credit terms you can track, and send monthly statements. Azeli PMS keeps company accounts with credit limits and aging so this does not turn into unpaid bills.

10. Sell events and packages

Conferences, workshops, weddings and retreats bring groups of rooms at once. Make it easy to see which function rooms are free on which dates, and quote packages that include rooms, meals and the hall.

Bring guests back

11. Follow up every guest

Ask for a review the day after check-out. Keep the guest's preferences. Ask permission to send them offers, and then send them a few times a year, not every week. A past guest who rebooks directly costs you nothing in commission.

Do not lose rooms you already have

12. Keep every room sellable

A room with a broken air conditioner or a leaking shower is a room you cannot sell. Report faults the moment they are found, block the room only for as long as needed, and track the repair. In Azeli PMS a blocked room comes off the website and OTAs at once and goes back on sale when housekeeping marks it fixed, so you neither sell a broken room nor forget a fixed one.

A 30-day plan

WeekFocus
1Pull 12 months of occupancy by weekday and channel. Fix the top OTA listing.
2Complete Google Business Profile, answer all reviews, move WhatsApp to a shared inbox.
3Set seasonal and non-refundable rates; contact ten local companies with a corporate rate.
4Send a first campaign to past guests who opted in; review results and plan next month.

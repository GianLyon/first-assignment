A property management system (PMS) is the software that runs your hotel: reservations, check-in, guest bills, housekeeping and, in many cases, the restaurant, stores and accounts. Choosing one is a decision you will live with for years, so it is worth getting right.

This guide is written by Azeli, which makes a PMS, so you should read it knowing that. We have tried to be fair: every system below is used successfully by hotels, and the right one depends on your property.

What a PMS should do for a Ugandan hotel

The basics are the same everywhere: reservations, a room board, check-in and check-out, guest bills and reports. In Uganda a few things matter more than in brochures written for Europe or the US:

  • Mobile money. MTN MoMo and Airtel Money are everyday payment methods. The system should record them as their own payment type and reconcile them, not lump them in as "other".
  • UGX amounts and local tax. Prices in shillings, VAT at 18%, and for VAT-registered businesses a plan for URA's EFRIS electronic receipts.
  • Payroll that knows PAYE and NSSF, if you want HR in the same system.
  • WhatsApp, because that is where most enquiries and guest messages arrive.
  • Unreliable power and internet. Cloud systems need a connection. Plan a backup (a second network or a mobile router) whatever you choose.
  • Local support in your time zone, from people who know how a Ugandan hotel works.

The checklist: questions to ask any vendor

  1. Does it connect to Booking.com, Expedia and Agoda through a channel manager, and is that included or extra?
  2. Can it record MTN and Airtel mobile money payments and reconcile them?
  3. How does it handle EFRIS e-receipts for VAT-registered hotels?
  4. Does it include a restaurant and bar POS, stores and accounting, or will I need other systems?
  5. What does it cost per month, in what currency, and what is extra (setup, training, channel manager, extra users)?
  6. Who trains my staff, and who do I call at 10pm when check-in stops working?
  7. Can I get all my data out if I leave?
  8. Can I give each staff member only the access they need?

Ask every vendor these questions, including us.

The main options

eZee Absolute (Yanolja Cloud Solution)

A cloud PMS widely used by independent hotels in Africa and Asia. It is mature, with a large set of modules: front desk, channel manager (eZee Centrix), booking engine and a restaurant POS. It suits hotels that want a well-known, proven product with many features. Check what local support is available near you and which modules are priced separately.

Cloudbeds

A cloud platform that bundles PMS, channel manager and booking engine, popular with small hotels, hostels and guesthouses worldwide. It is easy to learn and strong on distribution. It is less focused on deep back-office needs like stores, recipes or local payroll, so larger full-service hotels often pair it with other tools.

Oracle OPERA Cloud

The system used by many international chains and large hotels. It is extremely capable and integrates with almost everything, but it is priced, implemented and supported for larger properties. For a 30-room independent hotel it is usually more system, and more cost, than needed.

Hotelogix

A cloud PMS aimed at small and mid-size hotels and groups, with front desk, POS and a channel manager connection. A reasonable middle option; as with all of them, check the cost of the modules you actually need.

Spreadsheets, notebooks and local desktop software

Still common, and cheap at first. The hidden costs are double bookings, lost guest history, revenue that is hard to account for, and owners who cannot see what is happening without being on site.

Where Azeli PMS fits

Azeli PMS was built for the way hotels in Uganda and the region actually run. It is not the right choice for everyone, but it is a strong one if you are an independent hotel that wants more bookings and fewer systems:

  • Everything in one system: front desk, booking engine, OTA connection, WhatsApp inbox, housekeeping app, restaurant and bar POS, stores and purchasing, double-entry accounting, payroll with PAYE and NSSF, and events.
  • Mobile money and UGX built in: MTN and Airtel payments are their own methods with references, and reconcile against your statements.
  • An assistant for every department that lists what needs doing: bills to collect, accounts that do not balance, stock running out, staff records missing details, quiet weeks to fill.
  • WhatsApp with buying signals: each chat is scored so the front desk can tell a ready buyer from someone just asking around.
  • Access rights per person: the GM and finance see everything, department heads see their department, staff see only what they need.
  • Included with our marketing partnership, with setup, training and support from the same team that runs your marketing.

Honest limits: it is a cloud system, so you need a reliable connection; it is offered together with Azeli's marketing services rather than as stand-alone software; and if you are part of an international chain, your brand will usually require its own system.

How to choose

  1. List what you need today and in two years (rooms, outlets, events, payroll).
  2. Ask two or three vendors the checklist questions above, in writing.
  3. Try each system with your own staff for an afternoon, not just in a sales demo.
  4. Add up the full yearly cost, including the channel manager, training and support.

Why Azeli as your hotel marketing solution?

Most hotels do not have a marketing problem. They have a connection problem. The photos sit with one photographer, the OTA listings with whoever set them up years ago, the website with a developer who no longer answers, the WhatsApp line on the receptionist's phone, and the numbers in a spreadsheet nobody trusts. Each part works a little. Together they leak bookings.

Azeli Technologies was built to close those gaps. This article explains how we work, what you get, and, just as important, when we are not the right choice.

The problem with hotel marketing today

When we audit a property, we usually find the same five issues:

  1. Listings that undersell the hotel. Old photos, missing amenities, room names that do not match what the guest gets, and content that was never translated or optimised for how guests search.
  2. Too much dependence on OTAs. Booking.com and Expedia are valuable, but if most of your rooms come through them, you are paying commission on guests you could have reached directly.
  3. Slow replies. An enquiry on WhatsApp that waits three hours for an answer has usually booked somewhere else.
  4. No follow-up. Past guests are the cheapest bookings a hotel can get, yet most hotels never contact them again.
  5. No reliable numbers. Without occupancy, average rate and channel mix by month, every marketing decision is a guess.

What Azeli does differently

We work as your hotel's marketing and revenue team, not as an agency that sends a monthly report. In practice that means:

  • OTA listing and optimisation. We rebuild your listings on Booking.com, Expedia, Agoda and others: photos, descriptions, room mapping, policies, rate plans and review responses. Read more in OTA management, listing and optimisation made easy.
  • A website that takes bookings. Fast, mobile-first, found on Google and in AI answers, with a commission-free booking engine connected to live availability.
  • Photography and video that shows the rooms guests will actually stay in.
  • Guest messaging and CRM. WhatsApp and email that reach guests before, during and after their stay, and campaigns to past guests who agreed to hear from you.
  • Paid advertising on Google and Meta, only when the numbers say it will pay back.
  • Sales reporting every month: what was sold, through which channel, at what price, and what we will change next.

The full list is on our services page.

One team, one system

The part most hotels notice first is that Azeli comes with its own hotel software. Azeli PMS runs the front desk, reservations, OTA connection, WhatsApp inbox, housekeeping, restaurant and bar, stores, accounting, payroll and events in one place. It is included when you choose us as your marketing partner.

This matters for marketing because the same system that takes the booking also knows:

  • which weeks are quiet three weeks ahead, so we can run an offer before it is too late;
  • which WhatsApp enquiries are ready to book and which are only looking, so the front desk answers the right ones first;
  • which guests came back, how much they spent and whether they agreed to marketing;
  • how much of your revenue goes to OTA commission each month.

An assistant in the system lists what each department needs to do that day: guests to check out and bills to collect, stock running low, accounts that do not balance, and marketing gaps like a missing review link or a campaign still in draft.

OccupancyAre rooms being sold?

Who we are a good fit for, and who we are not

A good fit: independent hotels, lodges, guesthouses, serviced apartments and restaurants, usually between 8 and 150 rooms, that want more direct bookings and one partner to answer to.

Probably not a fit: large international chains with their own brand standards and corporate systems, or owners looking only for a one-off photo shoot. We are happy to point you to the right people in those cases.

Core modules

Every department, one system

Ten modules that share the same guests, rooms, stock and accounts, so information is entered once and flows everywhere it is needed.

01

Front Office

The front desk on one screen, with search for any booking, room or guest from the top bar.

  • Full booking form: returning-guest search, several rooms in one booking, per-night plan prices, company discounts, guarantee and deposit
  • Room board, availability grid and arrivals and departures
  • Check-in with registration card and ID details; room moves with a reason; stay extensions
  • Guest bills, charge to room, payments by cash, card, mobile money, bank or company account, with printable receipts
  • Night audit that posts room charges and marks no-shows
  • Invoices and registration cards printed on your letterhead
02

Booking Engine & Rates

Commission-free bookings from your own website, always showing live rooms and prices.

  • Rate plans with meal plans (room only to all inclusive), cancellation days, deposit, valid dates, arrival days and minimum and maximum stay
  • Rate calendar with seasonal prices and stop-sells
  • Guests book, look up and cancel online within your rules
  • Protected against selling the last room twice
  • A booking bar or button for the hotel's own website, in its colours
  • Online payment by card or MTN / Airtel mobile money through Pesapal
03

Channel Manager (OTAs)

Booking.com, Expedia, Agoda and more through a certified connection, so one sale closes the room everywhere.

  • Availability, prices, minimum stays and stop-sells pushed automatically
  • OTA bookings, changes and cancellations arrive in Reservations within a minute
  • Multi-room OTA bookings handled; overbookings flagged at once
  • OTA commission accrued every night in the accounts
  • OTA virtual cards listed with the dates they can be charged; the card number is never stored
04

Guests, WhatsApp & CRM

Every guest once, with their stays, spend, preferences and allergies, and their WhatsApp chats in the same place.

  • WhatsApp inbox for the front desk, linked to each guest's stay
  • Buying signals on every chat: explorer, interested, buyer or ready to book, with how sure and how specific
  • Automatic WhatsApps before arrival, on check-in, mid-stay, at check-out and after the stay
  • Email campaigns to past guests who opted in; pre-arrival and thank-you emails
  • Phone book of guests and suppliers, exportable to the front-desk phone
05

Housekeeping & Maintenance

Room attendants work from their phones; faulty rooms come off sale until they are fixed.

  • Daily room list from departures, stayovers and dirty rooms, arrivals first
  • Rooms shared out fairly; attendants tap Start, Done, Not now or Report fault
  • Supervisors inspect, pass or send rooms back
  • Room blocks: out of order or out of service, for set dates or until fixed, taken off the website and OTAs at once
  • Repair jobs, preventive servicing and the asset register
06

Restaurant & Bar POS

Point of sale for restaurants, bars and room service, linked to guest bills and stock.

  • Bills per table, walk-in or room service; orders sent to the kitchen and bar screens
  • Charge to room, cash, card, mobile money or split payment
  • Recipes take ingredients out of stock as you sell
  • Cashier shifts with counted cash-up; voids only by a manager, with a reason
07

Stores & Procurement

Know what is in every store, what it costs, and what is about to run out.

  • Purchase orders approved by someone else, part deliveries, weighted average cost
  • Departmental requisitions with approval and issue
  • Stock counts and write-offs posted to the accounts
  • Recipe costing and food cost reports
  • Suppliers with bank and mobile money details and payment terms
08

Accounting

Double-entry books fed automatically by the front desk, POS, stores, events and payroll. The database refuses any entry that does not balance.

  • Company accounts with credit limits, invoices, receipts and aging
  • Supplier bills and payments, expenses with VAT, transfers
  • Bank and mobile money reconciliation; closing the books by date
  • Profit & loss, balance sheet, general ledger and VAT summary
09

People & Payroll

Staff records, rosters, attendance, leave and Uganda payroll.

  • Staff records with NIN, TIN, NSSF and how they are paid
  • Rosters, attendance and leave with approval by someone else
  • Payroll with PAYE bands, NSSF, overtime and advances; payslips
  • Staff self-service for their own payslips and leave
10

Events & Banqueting

Conferences, weddings and functions, from the first enquiry to the final invoice.

  • Calendar of function rooms by day: held, enquiry or free
  • Quotes with packages, room hire and extras; no double booking
  • Deposits, banquet event orders for every department, invoicing
  • Amenities and services: spa, transfers, tours, sold to guests or walk-ins

Built into every module

The parts that save hours every day

An assistant for every department

Each morning it lists what needs doing: guests to check out and what to collect, accounts that do not balance, stock that is running out, staff records with missing details, quiet weeks to fill, WhatsApp leads ready to book. Optional AI answers questions in plain words and drafts replies for staff to send.

Dashboards & reports

Revenue by department, income against expenses, occupancy and a 14-day forecast. Department reports emailed automatically after each night audit; a group dashboard if you own several hotels.

Access rights for every person

Roles for the general manager, finance, department heads and staff, editable rights per role, and extra or removed rights for one person. Every action is in the audit trail.

Two-step sign-in & nightly backups

Codes from an authenticator app for the roles you choose, and an encrypted copy of your data stored off-site every night.

Charge to room

Restaurant, bar, spa, laundry and transfers go on the guest's bill as they happen and are settled together at check-out.

Works on any phone

A web system: nothing to install. The front desk on a computer, attendants and managers on their phones.

How it connects

One guest, one bill, zero double entry

Here is how a single stay moves through the system without anyone re-typing a thing.

  1. 1Check-in

    The guest checks in and a bill (folio) opens for room 204.

  2. 2Dinner

    They order dinner; the waiter charges it to room 204 on the POS.

  3. 3Spa

    A massage is booked at the spa and added to the same bill.

  4. 4Check-out

    One clear bill at check-out, paid by card, cash or mobile money.

  5. 5Books & stock

    Accounting, stock and reports update automatically.

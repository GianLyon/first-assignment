Core modules

Every department, one system

Eight core modules that share the same guests, rooms, stock and accounts, so information is entered once and flows everywhere it is needed.

01

Hotel Management & Front Office

Run the front desk from one screen: reservations, check-in and check-out, room assignments and guest bills, with housekeeping and the night audit built in.

  • Reservations, walk-ins and group bookings
  • Interactive room rack and booking calendar
  • Fast check-in and check-out with ID capture
  • Guest folios, split bills and charge to room
  • Housekeeping status and room maintenance
  • Night audit and daily close
02

Website Booking Engine

Commission-free bookings straight from your website, social media and Google, always showing your live rooms and rates.

  • Mobile-first booking on your own website
  • Live availability and rates from the PMS
  • Packages and add-ons offered at checkout
  • Promo codes and special rates
  • Card and mobile money payments
  • Instant confirmation emails

Restaurant / POS Software

Fast, simple point of sale for restaurants, bars, room service and pool bars, linked directly to guest bills.

  • Table plans, orders and split bills
  • Kitchen and bar order tickets
  • Charge to room, cash, card or mobile money
  • Menus, modifiers and happy-hour pricing
  • Stock deducted automatically for every recipe
  • Shift reports and cash-up

Inventory Management Software

Know what is in every store, kitchen and bar, what it costs and when to re-order.

  • Main store and department stores
  • Purchase orders and goods received
  • Supplier management and price tracking
  • Recipe costing for food and beverage
  • Stock transfers, counts and variances
  • Low-stock alerts and re-order levels
05

Human Resource Management

Everything about your team in one place, from hiring to payroll.

  • Staff records and contracts
  • Duty rosters and shift scheduling

  • Leave requests and approvals

Full double-entry accounting, fed automatically by the front desk, POS, inventory and payroll, so your books are always up to date.

How it connects

One guest, one bill, zero double entry

Here is how a single stay moves through the system without anyone re-typing a thing.

  1. 1Check-in

    The guest checks in and a bill (folio) opens for room 204.

  2. 2Dinner

    They order dinner; the waiter charges it to room 204 on the POS.

  3. 3Spa

    A massage is booked at the spa and added to the same bill.

  4. 4Check-out

    One clear bill at check-out, paid by card, cash or mobile money.

  5. 5Books & stock

    Accounting, stock and reports update automatically.

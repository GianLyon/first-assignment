FAQ

Questions we often hear

Do you only work with large hotels?

No. We work with independent hotels, boutique properties, guesthouses, serviced apartments and restaurants of every size. Packages scale to fit.

Can I hire you for just one service?

Absolutely. Many clients start with a photo shoot or an OTA optimisation and add more once they see results.

Will you help us reduce our reliance on OTAs?

Yes. OTAs are a valuable channel, so we optimise them, but we also build up your website, CRM and ads so more guests book directly with you and you pay less commission.

Which CRM and booking systems do you work with?

We're system-agnostic. We'll recommend options that fit your budget and integrate with your existing PMS, channel manager or reservation system.

What is GEO, and why does it matter?

GEO (generative engine optimisation) is making sure AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity know about your property and recommend it. More and more guests ask these tools where to stay or eat, so we make sure your website, listings and reviews give them clear, consistent information to work with.

How do you report results?

You get a clear monthly report covering bookings, revenue, ad spend, return on ad spend, listing performance and reviews, plus a call to walk through it.

Is there a long-term contract?

One-off projects (photography, website builds) are fixed-price. Ongoing services run month-to-month after an initial 3-month set-up period.
